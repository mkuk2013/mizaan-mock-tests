OUR MISSION

Mizaan Testing Service (MTS) exists to give every capable student in Sindh a fair, affordable and transparent way to test their knowledge and be rewarded for their academic performance.

Prepare for the real challenge with official-style online mock tests — built only on the Sindh Textbook Board, Jamshoro textbooks and the Sindh Board syllabus based on them, in the same MCQ format as the real test on 29 December 2026. No other board's syllabus is used.

ABOUT THE TEST
  • The test is based on multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
  • The syllabus follows the Sindh Textbook Board (STBB), Jamshoro textbooks only — no Punjab, Federal, Cambridge or any other board's syllabus.
  • The test will be conducted online.
  • The final, authorised result will be announced by MTS.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
  • Must be a resident of Sindh province only.
  • Enrolled in Class 8th, 9th, 10th or 12th.
  • Studying at a government or private school.
  • Last exam result must be above 70%.
  • One application per student only.
  • Incorrect or incomplete forms will be rejected.
MOCK TESTS — CHOOSE YOUR CLASS

Every class has 5 separate mock tests (100 MCQs each, 100 minutes) based only on the Sindh Textbook Board, Jamshoro textbooks — the same style as the real Sindh Academic Talent Challenge. Each class is password protected: tap any mock test, enter your class password once, and all 5 tests of that class stay unlocked on your device.

Syllabus — Sindh Textbook Board, Jamshoro only: Every MCQ in these mock tests is taken from the STBB Jamshoro textbook of your own class and subject, and the Sindh Board syllabus based on those textbooks. No question comes from the Punjab Textbook Board, Federal Board, Cambridge / O-Level, KPK or Balochistan syllabus.
